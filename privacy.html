Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Havok & Chaos Clips ("the app") is a desktop tool that posts clips to the Havok & Chaos accounts.

What we collect

When an account is connected through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) for the connected account only. This is used solely to upload videos and check their publishing status.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are never sold, shared, or sent to any server other than the platform's official API.

Viewers

The app does not collect data about people who watch our videos.

Deletion

Revoke access from your TikTok settings or message us and all stored tokens are deleted.
